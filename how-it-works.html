How It Works

From a few answers to a shortlist that fits.

No endless tabs, no “best of” lists. Three steps turn what you need into a ranked set of matches — with the reasoning shown.

The three steps

The same process runs in every category, so the result is always comparable — and always explainable.

  1. 01

    Tell us what you need

    You answer a small set of questions: what you’ll use it for, your budget, and the priorities you won’t compromise on. These questions are chosen because they actually change the decision.

  2. 02

    We narrow the options

    Your answers become requirements. Candidate products are compared against those requirements — not against popularity — and the ones that don’t fit fall away.

  3. 03

    See your matches

    You get a short list of matches with clear reasons — why each fits, and where it makes a trade-off — so the next decision is easy to make.

Your requirements

Budget, priorities and use case define the criteria every product is measured against.

+

Product information

Features, specifications and pricing are compared against those criteria.

Clear reasoning

Each match explains why it fits and where it involves a trade-off.

Get more out of it

The flow works better when you’ve done a little thinking first. Our guides walk you through the useful prep.

Before you start, it helps to be able to answer three things — even roughly:

  • What will you mainly use it for? The use case sets the bar for which features matter.
  • What’s your budget? A range is enough; you can adjust it later.
  • What do you refuse to compromise on? Naming one or two non-negotiables makes trade-offs obvious.

If you’re not sure how to answer yet, start with how to define what you need before you compare.
